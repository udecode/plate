Prop writes and observed-state publication are separate, preserving immediate prop transitions and sibling restrictions. The root's permanent contenteditable attribute is intentional selection policy; `aria-readonly` and the external input state are the relevant read-only oracles. No CodeMirror adapter behavior, public API, or serialized shape changes are needed for this repair.
