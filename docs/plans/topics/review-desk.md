# Review desk

Page: https://claude.ai/artifact/HLYerHCL1r65tGVNEqwUJM

How the owner reviews what pstack lands on next: the commits, their plans and decision logs, their proof and the chips around them.

Today that review runs through four separate tools. The terminal review script lists commits on next by session and marks them reviewed, but its marker was never set, so it shows only the last 20. Each hand-back links its commits on DiffsHub, one commit at a time. Plan pages show a plan's open questions and picks. The chips board prints the task queue. Nothing keeps a growing list of landed work to read, cuts it to the paths the owner cares about, or records which files and plan versions the owner read.
