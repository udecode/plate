# Rejected cuts and decided values

Page: https://claude.ai/artifact/NHiZFfaVWxKpJN8s6awMWL

What happens to property values when a reviewer rejects a proposed Enter or split, and when a decision lands a value on a node whose type or place changed. Plans `docs/plans/2026-10-20-cut-join-values.md`, `-order.md`, `-moves.md`, `docs/plans/2026-10-21-cut-join-classes.md` and `docs/plans/2026-10-21-cut-join-slices.md` are this subject's earlier iterations. `docs/plans/2026-10-21-cut-join-redesign.md` and `docs/plans/2026-10-21-cut-join-storage.md` followed them. `docs/plans/2026-10-22-carried-moves.md` built the storage plan's moves and landed with its storage and joins slices. `docs/plans/2026-10-23-giveback-order.md` added the decision-order check's give-back family, and `docs/plans/2026-10-23-giveback-copy.md` built the give-back. Eight iterations followed, from `docs/plans/2026-10-24-split-rejoin-order.md` to `docs/plans/2026-10-27-split-rejoin-order-8.md`. They tried to make a rejected Enter rejoin the same way in every click order, and the eighth is the last iteration so far.

## Main changes

- `joinRejectedCuts` in `packages/plitejs/src/authored/tree/cuts.ts` joins a rejected cut's halves without comparing their values, and the joined element keeps the left half's properties. A block join keeps the left block's type and properties.
- Two owners write decided values. `settleFormats` in `packages/plitejs/src/authored/facts/settle.ts` restores a pending tag's written value at admission, and `decideFormats` in `packages/plitejs/src/authored/tree/resolve.ts` writes each decided value during a decision. Both pass the node through `heldProps` in `packages/plitejs/src/authored/tree/holds.ts`, which keeps only the values the node's place declares and accepts. A decision that retypes, wraps or unwraps a node judges it, and the children it lifts, at the new place.
- A decision that would drop a value waits instead while a pending suggestion still decides that place: a retype of the node, or a wrap, unwrap, join, split or retype of an ancestor. It returns blocked with that suggestion.
- Yjs import, in `canonicalize` in `packages/plitejs/src/yjs/core/plugin.ts`, drops a merged value its place refuses, so concurrent edits give every peer the same document instead of an admission error.
- A producer that relocates a node records where it stood, and `formerPlace` in `packages/plitejs/src/core/change/lineage.ts` reads that place back for the current command. A write that carries a value its new place cannot hold drops it only when `carriesDefault` in `packages/plitejs/src/core/default-storage.ts` finds it was the left-out default there and no other change is pending on that key or on the node's place; any other value still refuses. In Suggesting, `carriedJudge` in `packages/plitejs/src/authored/tree/carried.ts` writes the drop as a removal tag, so rejecting gives the value back.
- A proposed inline split copies the left half's format tags on each key it drops onto the right half, under one tag of the split's change whose `split` field marks them as that split's history, and writes no `kept`. When the split is rejected and the right half's own writes leave only the split's output, `decideKey` in `packages/plitejs/src/authored/tree/layers.ts` reads the copies as stored, and reports that read to the joins comparison in `packages/plitejs/src/authored/tree/halves.ts`. So a rejected cut gives back what it dropped in every decision order, whether or not its left half stands. Halves that stay apart drop the copies, so a dropped id never stands on both. Halves in two roots read as halves in two blocks do: outside a schema with content roots, `resolveAuthoredValue` finds a left half in another root, and it keeps a dropped key to itself while it stands. A rejected cut whose halves sit in two blocks, in either order, or in two roots waits on a pending removal of its left half's block, since that removal never waits on the cut, while the left half's own removal waits on the cut. A left half that a decision removes passes its cut to the fragment before it, as `relinkCuts` does once a removal applies. Once a rejected cut's left half stands apart, every fragment that a later split copied that cut's tags onto drops them too. A cut saved before this change gives nothing back once its left half is gone.
- `splitCopiesOf` in `packages/plitejs/src/authored/tree/layers.ts` keys each copy by its key and write id, since a write id is unique only on one node and key. Halves that end in one block after the decision, such as through an accepted join, read as met and take the copies back. The walk to the fragment before a removed one crosses pending and rejected cuts. It stops at an accepted cut, because accepting a cut removes the tags that link its pieces. When a rejected cut's left half goes by its own removal, the right half takes back what the cut dropped, unless another piece of that left half still stands. The walk goes back from the left half through each split made in it since the cut, accepted or rejected, past every piece that is gone. A piece that stands holds the value the copies would give back, so the right half keeps its own write and drops the copies. While a removal of that piece is pending, the left half's removal waits on it. A fragment drops only the copies of the cuts it descends from, found by walking from its own cuts to their left fragments, so a write id that one update shares with an unrelated link's split never strips the fragment's own write. Removing a pending cut's left fragment waits on the cuts the fragment starts while all of them are pending, and deciding one of them waits on a pending removal of the fragment, since either verdict can drop the tags that tie the fragment's pieces.
