## Main changes

- **Each open question is a decision memo.** The question line ends in a question mark, then `Why it needs you:`, the facts as plain bullets, each option as `- **<label>**: <what happens> Cost: <what it costs>`, `Why I pick it:` when one option is recommended, and `If you say go:` only when none is. Needs you renders each one as a numbered card with that order, the pick marked and its reason under it.
- **The renderer refuses an open plan's question that skips a part.** It names the missing part. An executed plan, or one with no state word, renders the parts it has, and a question with no options renders as plain text.
- **Color marks what to act on.** Needs you takes the color of its most urgent question, and the call-to-action color is orange, not pink. Status words, review seats, finding severities and results each take a hue from a 12-hue palette that reads at 5:1 or better in both themes. In Needs you and the header only badges have a background, as the owner asked; code blocks and diffs keep their fills.
- **The header shows the pstack flow.** A rail of the ten pstack stages, from Plan to Reflect, sits above the `Status:` sentence, which says what the plan waits on, such as a commit you owe. It reads the decision log's stage phases, the Steps boxes and the Status, and the block's Plans and trails rule tells agents to log each stage under its phase. Seat chips drop `codex:` and show the effort as plain text.
- **The rail names the playbook and the pstack skills of five stages.** A plan's `Playbook:` line names the playbook poteto-mode picked, either a project playbook or pstack's. The Plan stage shows it, with the pstack playbooks a project playbook extends. The review stages show `interrogate`, Audit shows `show-me-your-work`, and Design and Writing keep their tool badges. Badges show only once a stage ran, and Build, Verify, Ship and Reflect carry none. The Proof stage is now Verify. The renderer refuses a `Playbook:` name that is neither a project playbook nor pstack's, and sync-pstack's `verify` fails when pstack adds a playbook the renderer does not list.
- **The page is compact.** The brief is one card with two rows, Changes and Risks, and the type and spacing are tighter. The renderer refuses an open plan whose brief still asks Found, Your call or On go, and a closed plan's older brief shows only its Changes and Risks.
- **Memos have word budgets.** Each part runs at most 15 words, an option's label at most 3, and a memo holds at most 2 facts, in plain words with no file paths, commands or hashes. The renderer refuses an open plan's memo that runs past a budget and names the part. When an option is recommended, go takes it, so the memo has no go line.
- **The page shows what a reviewer judges.** It shows the rail, the `Status:` sentence, the two answers, Needs you, Picked for you, the Public API pairs, the playbook's review sections and Main changes, with nothing folded. A review section the plan lacks comes from the subject, marked current. One line names the plan and subject files, which keep the proof, steps, history and review rounds. A page without a brief shows its whole record with every section open.
- **You answer with clicks.** Each question is a row of radios, or checkboxes for a `Pick any.` question, with my pick checked. A sticky bar says go while every pick stands, and gives a reply to copy when you change one, such as go, except Last fixes: Review first.
- **Each question shows how much it needs you.** A plan states `Attention: safe`, `look` or `answer`, and the question's number turns green, amber or red. Red questions come first, the head counts each color, and the frame takes the most urgent one. A question with no pick is always red.
- **Agents read pages locally.** An agent reads a page from its rendered file under the plans directory or from the plan and subject files, never from the published claude.ai page, so a switched account cannot block it. A page is published again only while its work is active.
- **Workflow guides live in the repos.** Ellie gets `docs/development/agent-workflow.md`, plate-2 keeps `docs/development/agent-skills.md`, and both `AGENTS.md` files point there instead of at a claude.ai page.
