# Sweep round 1

Status: planning: ready for the round to start on live beta
Playbook: plan

## Brief

### What will change?

Round 1 now has its test table: 104 pages split between natamox and Sin, each with a browser and a time limit, plus 9 rechecks of known bugs. Pages for plugins still being refactored wait for a later round.

### What could go wrong?

We skip the test of whether the six set moves find more bugs than free clicking, so we still won't know. A bug on a held page stays hidden until its plugin's refactor lands.

## Teach

A round plan is the sheet the testers work from. Each row is one page: who tests it, in which browser, for how many minutes, and which page it is compared with. After each session the tester fills in the minutes used, the recording and a one-line note for each thing that looked wrong.

The sweep plan expected a hidden copy of beta with old bugs put back, so that half the pages would use the six moves and the other half free clicking. That copy is not coming, so every page runs on live beta with free clicking.

This round also holds back the pages of plugins whose refactor has not landed, because the refactor would throw away a fix made there. A plugin counts as refactored once its review is marked done in the review ledger.

## Outcome

Every page in the Sessions table gets one recorded session in its browser, each recheck is marked pass, find or blocked, and each P5 area has its notes. Then each find is rechecked on live beta and drafted, and the sweep plan's step 1.10 fixes them.

## Scope

- 104 sessions: 22 P1, 54 P2, 22 P3 and 6 P4, one of them the Demo page, whose pairing is still open.
- 25 held P2 pages, listed in Held pages, each waiting for its plugin's review in the review ledger to close.
- 9 rechecks and one held recheck.
- Four P5 areas and the iPhone page checks on the 22 P1 pages.

Out of scope: the planted-bug trial and the six moves, which wait for a later round; fixes, which run in the sweep plan's step 1.10.

## Decisions

**The trial.** natamox confirmed on 2026-10-19 that the sweep plan's step 1.6 will not run, so the trial is inconclusive, as that step's fallback says. Every page gets free clicking on live beta, the Arm column is gone, and finds can be drafted as soon as they are rechecked.

**Which pages run.** natamox asked to test only refactored plugins. The review ledger gives each plugin area one review scope, and a closed scope means its review ended and any refactor it chose has landed. A scope still open as pursue-not-adopted, deferred or a fork has a refactor coming. Nine such scopes own the held pages: ai, autocomplete, date, diff, markdown, search, styles, suggestions and toc. The lead matched each docs page to the review scope whose owners or members list its demo files. The core scopes that are still open, runtime, selection, transactions and DOM lookup names, hold no page, because every page runs on them.

**Who tests.** The split page gave natamox every P1 and P3 page and the P2 pages near natamox's recent fixes, and gave Sin the other Plate pages. After holding 25 pages, natamox had about an hour more than Sin before the iPhone and P5 work. Moving the controlled editor, the collaboration example, Yjs and the server-side example to Sin leaves 386 session minutes for natamox and 390 for Sin.

**Browsers.** Each page's browser comes from its place in the full page list: the first page gets Chrome, the second Safari, the third Firefox, the fourth the iPhone, and so on. Each tester covers all four browsers, and the 25 held pages keep their turn.
