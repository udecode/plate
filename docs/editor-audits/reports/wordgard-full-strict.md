# Full Wordgard audit — preserved conclusion

Historical summary of the locally recovered report. The original artifact may
be absent from another checkout. Its SHA-256 binds this summary to the report
read during preservation, not to current product source or rerun proof.
The [audit registry](../index.json) retains the complete original provenance.

The July 27 audit preferred the local stack for 96 of 101 evaluated concepts:
42 Plite, 29 Plate, and 25 combined-stack preferences. One reference advantage,
one equivalent result, two tradeoffs, and one insufficient-evidence result
prevented a blanket superiority claim.

It rejected a second document/state/view stack, blanket mobile keydown bypasses,
default central-authority collaboration, and decoration-driven editability.
Wordgard's localization ergonomics were stronger but belonged in application UI.
Its custom bidi engine remained a tradeoff; tile rendering lacked a common
benchmark and a proven local non-React consumer.

Two proof packets were material: repairing a stale clipboard benchmark caller
and producing raw mobile input-phase evidence. Runtime changes remained
conditional on a failure after proof repair. The audit did not authorize a
Wordgard architecture transplant.

Reported source coverage: 120 files, 19,110 TypeScript declaration nodes, and
101 atomic concepts, with exact exclusions and no unexplained captured entries.
Local strict verification passed types/package tests, then failed contracts
because the clipboard benchmark called unavailable getOptions; Chromium
closure did not run after fail-fast. Raw mobile plumbing was also unrunnable.

The harvest covered 27 files and 644 test sites. Android/iOS behavior, tile
performance, universal bidi superiority, and issue coverage remained unknown;
the issue provider was unsupported at that time. Historical failure and unknown
states stay intact. This summary is not a fresh strict-gate or device result.
