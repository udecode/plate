# Plate UI execution — preserved closure receipt

This summarizes the recovered final report for the
[46-family extraction plan](../../plans/2026-09-07-full-plate-ui-extraction-audit.md).
It preserves the report's completed local execution claim and its limits.
The original report and raw receipts may be absent in another checkout. No
product check, browser replay, or source-freshness validation ran during this
preservation pass; this is not current-release certification.

The final report accounts for all 637 original public dispositions:
425 retained/implemented, 38 made private, 62 removed/deleted, five localized/
inlined, 96 Plate forwarders removed while retaining canonical Plite identity,
and 11 moved to headless owners. Sixteen justified additions and seven further
cuts were recorded separately without changing the original denominator.

The final direction kept semantic sessions, current-node eligibility, and
atomic updates in feature owners. Copied presentation, transport, and
application discussion data stayed local. A proposed whole command-menu-fork
deletion was withdrawn after the replacement lacked three required imperative
controls. Those controls were retained; hypothetical replacement capability
did not count as a successful cut.

The report preserves their failed runs and records focused repairs followed by
fresh final gates. A log ending at either failure is not completion evidence.
