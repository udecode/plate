# ProseMirror: issues and regressions

## Issue corpus shape

- **ProseMirror's issue dispositions are keyword routing, and most of its skips were never reviewed.** `c70bacbd4a:docs/editor-issue-harvester/prosemirror/full/classify-issues.mjs:112-286` matches twelve cluster patterns against each issue's title, labels, body and every comment at once, and keeps the first match in a fixed priority order: IME and beforeinput, mobile and touch (deferred), clipboard and DOM parser or serializer, collaboration and step mapping, history, tables, shadow DOM, selection and drag-drop, decorations and NodeViews, model, schema and transforms, performance (deferred), then product policy (`plate-owned`, whose words include menus, input rules, `schema-list`, Tiptap, BlockNote and framework names). A match in the title wins. When the title matches no cluster, any support, docs or package, product-integration or repo-infrastructure word anywhere in the text, such as 'how to', 'help', 'version', 'build', 'react' or 'toolbar', skips the issue before its body is tried, and an issue that matches nothing is skipped as `uncertain-nonportable`. That early step made 372 of the 391 classifier skips, including bug reports such as #254 'Textblocks with an empty TextFragment cause problems', #288 'Editor freezes browser after few changes in IE11@Win7' and #318, an empty `markRange` that throws a RangeError. The text of 324 of those 372 also matched an editor cluster, a count that overstates the misses because the patterns include common words such as 'select', 'return' and 'position'. The closure overrides later reviewed all 1,029 non-skip rows and moved 190 of them to skip, but touched only 30 of the 391 classifier skips, and the ledger builder checks every skip row off as `invalid-skip`, so 361 of the ledger's 572 `invalid-skip` rows are unreviewed keyword verdicts. Evidence: `docs/editor-issue-harvester/prosemirror/full/classified-issues.json` (`cluster` and `secondaryClusters`, recounted 2026-10-08), `docs/editor-issue-harvester/prosemirror/full/issue-closure-overrides.json` (1,059 entries), `docs/editor-issue-harvester/prosemirror/full/build-closure-ledger.mjs:15-20`, `docs/editor-issue-harvester/prosemirror/full/issue-closure-ledger.md:12-30`. Limit: the comments come from the raw dump under `.tmp` that the classifier read, which a fresh checkout lacks (unpinned); 1,349 of its 1,420 issues carried comments.

- **Where the issues cluster.** The classifier's first-match clusters for the 1,420 ProseMirror issues (`c70bacbd4a:docs/editor-issue-harvester/prosemirror/full/matrix.md:17-37`): selection, DOM and caret 312; support questions 182; clipboard, paste and serialization 176; docs, release and packaging 174; model, schema and transforms 121; decorations, NodeViews and inline voids 105; mobile touch keyboards 81; IME, beforeinput and keyboard 74; plugin policy that maps to Plate 54; history 37; collaboration rebase 29; tables 20; uncertain or non-portable 19; product integration 14; large-document performance 12; shadow DOM 8; repo infrastructure 2. After the closure overrides the ledger's cluster column differs slightly, for example selection-dom-caret 308 and clipboard 177 (`docs/editor-issue-harvester/prosemirror/full/issue-closure-ledger.tsv`, recounted 2026-10-08). These are keyword-routed buckets, as this section's first bullet explains, so they show where reports' wording falls, not verified defect counts.

- **Harvest scope and closure.** The ProseMirror issue harvest (`docs/editor-issue-harvester/prosemirror/full/`) covers every issue of `ProseMirror/prosemirror`: 1,420 issues, 111 open and 1,309 closed, as refreshed at 2026-07-25T22:59:04Z through gitcrawl 0.5.0 over GitHub REST and checked against GitHub GraphQL (two issues the provider missed were verified on the host). ProseMirror is MIT (`../prosemirror/LICENSE`). Plite was the only patch target; Plate-owned rows were deferred, not patched. The generated closure ledger `issue-closure-ledger.tsv` closes every row, at 572 invalid-skip, 527 deferred-with-owner, 194 covered-by-existing-test and 127 test-written (`docs/editor-issue-harvester/prosemirror/full/issue-closure-ledger.md:22-29`); how many of the invalid-skip rows were never reviewed is in this section's first bullet. Its exact-test column points into the retired `.tmp/plite` checkout; most of those tests now live under `apps/plite/tests/plite-browser/donor/examples/` and `packages/plitejs/test/`, and some were renamed or removed.
