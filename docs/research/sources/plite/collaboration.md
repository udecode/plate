## Yjs structural edits

- **Repeated split and merge once leaked Yjs virtual placeholders.** In the June 2026 binding, two `Enter` then `Backspace` cycles at a paragraph end threw `Cannot merge Yjs nodes of different kinds` and left an extra empty paragraph on every peer, in 3 of 3 runs of the Yjs example. The note's hypothesis, from the June code: an element `merge_node` inserted a virtual move placeholder into the previous Yjs node and hid the merged target, a later element `split_node` cloned the raw Yjs children, placeholder included, because `getYjsChildren` filtered hidden nodes but not placeholders, and the reader then exposed the placeholder as an empty element, which an example that renders every element as `<p>` could show as nested paragraphs. Per-operation contracts missed it because none repeated split and merge while a placeholder remained. Plite's binding still keeps virtual placeholders as internal bookkeeping (`plite:yjs-virtual-placeholder`, `packages/plitejs/src/yjs/core/document.ts:42`, `:427`), and `packages/plitejs/test/yjs/split-merge-contract.spec.ts:136-220` pins repeated cycles and placeholder content through splits and merges. Source: `docs/research/raw/slate-v2/donor/autoresearch.research/yjs-pr21/notes/virtual-placeholder-split-merge-bug.md:1-145`, 2026-06-03, with `.tmp/slate-v2` paths that no longer exist.

- **Seeded multi-peer soaks find what per-operation contracts miss.** A June 2026 four-peer browser soak of the Yjs example recorded 15 reproducible sequences that mix offline and online, split, merge, wrap, unwrap, lift, move, set and unset role, reconcile, and undo or redo: an offline split undo plus a remote split converged to `["Hello world!", "world!"]` instead of `["Hello ", "world!"]`, and a redo variant duplicated `world!` again; leaf paths resolved to a paragraph, a role-title paragraph or a block quote and unmounted every editor root; lookups hit `No Yjs node at path 1.0`; the root lost its start or end text node; and rendering produced `<p>` inside `<p>` or a placeholder `<div>` inside `<p>`. The seeded cases now run in `packages/plitejs/test/yjs/structural-soak-contract.slow.ts:659-1050` (structural-mix seeds 42, 43, 46, 49 and 55, random-control seed 42 and others), and the offline split undo and redo cases in `packages/plitejs/test/yjs/split-node-contract.spec.ts:258` and `:277`. Source: `docs/research/raw/slate-v2/donor/autoresearch.research/yjs-pr21/notes/yjs-collaboration-soak-findings-2026-06-03.md:1-371`.
