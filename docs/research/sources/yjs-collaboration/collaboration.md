# Yjs collaboration: collaboration

## Operation replay

- **slate-yjs operation replay.** slate-yjs runs 54 fixtures through one runner (`BitPhinix/slate-yjs@69f3e0eaec:packages/core/test/index.test.ts:25-65`). The runner applies a legacy Slate operation to a local editor bound to a `Y.XmlText` and checks that the shared root normalizes back to the editor's children. It then applies the encoded update to a remote doc built from the pre-edit state, and asserts that the remote children equal the local children and the fixture's expected value. The families are addMark 5, insertNode 3, insertText 11, mergeNode 5, moveNode 8, removeMark 3, removeNode 4, removeText 3, setNode 6 and splitNode 6. They include nested blocks and four Unicode fixtures (counted at that commit on 2026-10-08). The report's "Local Plite operations" is rename damage for legacy Slate operations (`docs/editor-test-harvester/yjs-collaboration/report.md:70`). Plite tests the same node families over Yjs, one contract per change kind in `packages/plitejs/test/yjs/`: canonical-change (insert and remove text, insert node, replace children), split-node, merge-node, move-node, set-node (text mark `set_node` included), remove-node, insert-fragment, lift, wrap and unwrap. Each applies an offline local change and checks convergence with concurrent remote edits after reconnect. `packages/plitejs/test/collab-history-runtime-contract.ts` converges three peers across text, mark, delete and move commits by replaying serialized changes without Yjs. The Plate Yjs slow suite that the report targeted was deleted on 2026-06-24 (`b86f5902be:packages/yjs/src/lib/__tests__/collaboration/fixtures.ts`).

## Unicode and surrogate pairs

- **Unicode and surrogate pairs.** slate-yjs replays text with accented letters, a snowman, the surrogate-pair emoji 💩 and U+FEFF through insertText, removeText, splitNode and mergeNode (`BitPhinix/slate-yjs@69f3e0eaec:packages/core/test/collaboration/insertText/withUnicode.tsx` and its three `withUnicode.tsx` siblings). yjs `testSplitSurrogateCharacter` (`yjs/yjs@da05230083:tests/y-text.tests.js:1779`, for yjs issue #248; Yjs 14 prerelease) has a disconnected peer split a surrogate pair by inserting inside it, deleting half of it and formatting half of it. It then asserts that the update still encodes and both peers converge (`docs/editor-test-harvester/yjs-collaboration/report.md:71`). No Plite or Plate test that runs edits through a `Y.Doc` uses non-ASCII text or a Unicode escape. A search on 2026-10-08 covered every test file that imports yjs under `packages/plitejs`, `packages/platejs`, `apps/www` and the Plite browser examples. The emoji cases in `packages/platejs/src/ai/react/AIChatPlugin.suggestions.spec.ts` do not use Yjs, and the seeded text in `collaborative-history-contract.slow.ts` and `schema-identity-contract.spec.ts` is ASCII letters. This is an open test gap inferred from that search, not from a coverage run; no defect is known.

## Suggestions

- **Suggestions over Yjs.** y-prosemirror keeps suggestion-mode edits, including an Enter split, out of the base document that a peer without suggestions sees, and shows them with insertion and deletion marks to peers that view suggestions (`yjs/y-prosemirror@9200946f0e:tests/suggestions.test.js:124`, :544). It requires peers in the same mode to converge: :1012 and :1144 are delta-debug reductions of fuzz failures where suggestion viewers diverged after a split or a split and delete interleave. A 30-step randomized run across six users in three modes must also converge (`yjs/y-prosemirror@9200946f0e:tests/suggestion-simulation.test.js:203`, :232; Yjs 14 prerelease; `docs/editor-test-harvester/yjs-collaboration/report.md:74`). The report's "not raw Plite substrate" no longer holds. Plite's authored layer (`packages/plitejs/src/authored/`) tests proposals over Yjs in `packages/plitejs/test/yjs/authored-contract.spec.ts`. In 'merges independent proposals from disconnected peers', both peers keep their base children unchanged while their proposed views show both edits. Another test is 'keeps concurrent opposite decisions conflicted on both peers'. Plate's suggestion plugin reads that layer through `AuthoredPlugin` (`packages/platejs/src/features/suggestion/BaseSuggestionPlugin.ts:1-18`). Whether Plite covers y-prosemirror's split, delete and format interleaves was not checked.

## Late sync and out-of-order updates

- **Late sync and out-of-order updates.** yjs pins three rules (Yjs 14 prerelease):
  - Merged updates equal applying them one by one (`yjs/yjs@da05230083:tests/updates.tests.js:105`).
  - An update applied before its dependency is held as pending and resolves once the dependency arrives (:298).
  - Snapshots restore earlier states, including from either peer after both edited (`yjs/yjs@da05230083:tests/snapshot.tests.js:9`, :36, :164).

  When the harvest ran (plan `docs/plans/2026-05-13-yjs-collaboration-harvest.md`), Plate's slow Yjs suite named four such cases: reconnect_eventually_converges, concurrent_local_edits_while_disconnected_eventually_converge, out_of_order_updates_eventually_converge and timeout_then_late_sync_does_not_reseed (`b86f5902be:packages/yjs/src/lib/__tests__/collaboration/fixtures.ts:288-457`). That suite was deleted on 2026-06-24 in 888f9ccaed (`docs/editor-test-harvester/yjs-collaboration/report.md:76`). Plite now covers offline edits that reconnect against concurrent remote edits for each change kind, such as 'reconnects, undoes, and redoes insert_text while preserving remote edits' (`packages/plitejs/test/yjs/canonical-change-contract.spec.ts`). The test 'preserves three authors through every … delivery order and a move' (`packages/plitejs/test/yjs/exclusive-property-contract.spec.ts`) applies three authors' updates in every order. No current test is named for pending updates or for a late joiner that must not reseed (test names searched 2026-10-08), so those two old cases may be untracked (inferred).
