# Lexical: issues and regressions

## Issue corpus shape

- **Lexical's issues concentrate in clipboard, selection and tables.** A keyword classifier run over all 2,741 open and closed `facebook/lexical` issues numbered up to #8623 files 421 under clipboard, paste and HTML or markdown serialization, 416 under DOM selection, caret and focus, 248 under table selection and navigation, 71 under decorator, image, mention and other inline-atom boundaries, 66 under mobile keyboards, 65 under IME and beforeinput, 63 under collaboration, 41 under large-document performance, 38 under undo history and 8 under shadow DOM. Another 468 are playground and plugin product issues, such as lists, links, headings, formatting, code blocks, hashtags, toolbar and typeahead, and the other 836 are skipped as docs, release, support, build, Lexical-API, unclear or low-signal. Table issues bunch in #6346-#7419: 85 of those 400 issues, against 4 to 21 in each of the other twelve batches (200 issues each, 141 in the last), and most of the spike's titles concern merged cells, nested or scrollable tables, and table copy, paste or delete. Limit: these are first-match keyword counts, not reviewed labels. The classifier matches regular expressions on the title and labels, which decided 1,821 of the 1,905 non-skip rows, and keeps the first match in a fixed priority order (IME, mobile, table, collaboration, history, clipboard, selection, atoms, shadow DOM, performance, plugin), except that a title naming a toolbar, playground, plugin, extension, typeahead or similar product surface goes to the plugin bucket unless an IME, mobile, table, collaboration, history or clipboard pattern also matched. When the title and labels match nothing, it skips the issue as docs, support or build if any of its text holds a word such as "version", "help", "request" or "package", before it tries the body against the editor clusters; 702 of the 836 skips came from that step. A recheck on 2026-10-08 against the raw body corpus the classifier read, which a fresh checkout lacks (unpinned), found 352 of those 702 bodies matching an editor-cluster pattern, so the editor counts above are low. The later per-issue closure ledger reviewed only the 1,905 non-skip rows, moved 126 of them to skip and carried the 836 classifier skips over unreviewed, for 962 irrelevant in all.
