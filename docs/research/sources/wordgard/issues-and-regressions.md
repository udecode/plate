# Wordgard: issues and regressions

## Issue corpus shape

- **Wordgard's tracker is small and clusters on inline content, DOM mapping and redraw.** On 2026-09-09 Wordgard's Forgejo tracker (`https://code.haverbeke.berlin/wordgard/wordgard/issues`) held 73 issues, 12 of them open (docs/editor-issue-harvester/wordgard/full/issues.md:3, 9-81). By title, the largest groups are caret and DOM position mapping around inline plots, widgets, superscripts and nested content holes (#19-#21, #25, #38, #39, #47, #50, #53, #57, #62, #67, #71, #73); decoration and attribute redraw (#16-#18, #30, #36, #44, #63); mobile and IME input on Android and iOS (#3, #4, #8, #23, #24, #52); and mapping or ordering defects in the packed `RangeSet` and `PointSet` (#31, #54-#56, plus the open request #66 for overlapping ranges), the containers Plite rejected in favour of structural anchors. Smaller groups are exceptions that stop input or mutation processing (#15, #26, #28, #29), corrections that disturb selection or history (#40, #48, #65), table and list structure (#45, #60, #68) and a cache that retains every resolved document (#61); the rest are menu and link-dialog UI, examples, build and packaging. Limit: #1-#35 carry semantic decisions in `docs/editor-issue-harvester/wordgard/full/issue-closure-ledger.md`, while #36-#73 were listed by title only and never read, so their groups are leads, not confirmed defects.

- **Reading Wordgard's first 35 issues refined its audit more than it extended it.** On 2026-08-01 issues #1-#35 were read with their bodies and full timelines against `wordgard/wordgard@c715d4ded8fc780f52c13206e589ea31e4148dd4`: 22 were fixed, 12 open, 1 stale and none duplicate. As closures, 32 were deferred with an owner, 2 were invalid skips and #23 (an extra space at the start of a line after iOS swipe input) needs a raw-device repro; no existing Plite or Plate test covered any of them. The reading changed the Wordgard architecture audit for 20 issues, mostly by downgrading completeness or proof claims, such as mobile input with a source fix but no device proof, or decoration, widget and parser fixes whose only observed run was a stale `dist` build. Only #13 (no completion provider, query, accept or cancel lifecycle) and #27 (active key bindings not discoverable to pointer, keyboard or screen-reader users) added a concept the audit lacked, and the audit kept #13's completion requirement with Plate's feature owners, not editor core. Evidence: `docs/editor-issue-harvester/wordgard/full/issue-closure-ledger.md:13-29`, `:45-69`; the builder pins these counts at `docs/editor-issue-harvester/wordgard/full/build-closure-ledger.mjs:190-236`. Limit: the audit's concept matrix (`matrix-truth.mjs`) sits in an ignored run directory, so a fresh checkout can neither rerun the builder nor check the concept mapping.
