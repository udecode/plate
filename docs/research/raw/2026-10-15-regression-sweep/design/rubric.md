# Rubric for the cross-judge (candidates never see this)

Score each candidate 0 to 3 on each criterion, with one line of evidence each.

1. Owner priority. It makes the human P1 to P6 pass the main route, leaves agent-run testing for later, and turns each find into a fix plus the missing or corrected test at the lowest layer that sees it.
2. Repository consistency. A thin skill plus a project playbook with anchored deltas over a real pstack playbook, as `verdict` does; plan pages and decision logs; quotes that exist in the pstack playbook it extends.
3. Reuse, no duplication. It calls `verify`, issue-draft, the Bug fix playbook and the knowledge tool instead of restating them, adds no second law format and no hand-synced list.
4. Retirement that works. A concrete, checkable way a scenario names the test that covers it and drops out of the next round, so manual work falls.
5. Aim. Scenarios come from concrete evidence of where agents missed before, and the design avoids a full checklist of every gesture on every page.
6. Small and trialable. The fewest new files and concepts that reach the outcomes, and a fair trial against the incumbent and the simplest alternative before adoption.

Then recommend a base and name one or two grafts from the others, each with its candidate label.
