## Human pass over beta

For a hands-on pass that checks beta pages against a reference page, prepare at most five checks per page with the Scenario Generator above. Aim each check at one kind of bug in `docs/research/raw/2026-10-15-regression-sweep/census/census-a-summary.md` and `census-b-summary.md`, the bugs that reached beta while their tests passed. Each check names its page, its reference page, the browsers or devices, the starting state, the steps, what should happen, what must not happen and an estimate in minutes.
