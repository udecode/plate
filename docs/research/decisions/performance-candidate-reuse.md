# Reuse performance candidate evidence

Begin performance research with both recorded iterations. The first study
produced 34 conditional experiments and proposed lazy history recovery
projection as the first supported adoption candidate. Iteration 2 reconciles
material prior candidates and keeps Compiler and virtualization analyses
separate.

Each candidate's dossier owns its verdict, rejected alternatives, source
identity and reopening conditions. Neither iteration supplies an aggregate
architecture winner or proof of production adoption. Budget failures remain
failures; incomparable hosts and cohorts remain incomparable.

Use `node tooling/scripts/review-ledger.mjs research <key-or-term>` to locate
earlier lead/read/rejection rows. Read the corresponding candidate and receipt
before spending another investigation on it. A new source or proof family may
justify reopening; a repeated review request also permits fresh reasoning
without another broad external crawl.

[The ledger](../reviews.md#performance) records review order and source entry
points. Benchmark retains measurement, paired comparison and adoption gates.
