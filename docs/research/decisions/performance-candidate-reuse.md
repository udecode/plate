# Reuse performance candidate evidence

Pursue executable receipt admission and assessment at the existing measurement
owners. A stored success label does not establish correct output, complete
observations, current source or acceptance under the selected policy. Validate
those facts before applying an identified, frozen policy. Keep historical
availability, evidence validity, budgets, material improvement and diagnostic
attribution distinct.

The [September 30 review](../probes/2026-09-30-performance-review/REPORT.md)
reproduces false-positive S5 verdicts and aggregation of mismatched-source
artifacts. Consolidate duplicated active target definitions and active S5
evaluator code. Preserve independent artifact adapters, measurement domains,
original receipts and private Plite instrumentation. A universal benchmark
platform or public profiling API has no demonstrated job.

Begin performance research with both recorded iterations. The first study
produced 34 conditional experiments and proposed lazy history recovery
projection as the first supported adoption candidate. Iteration 2 reconciles
material prior candidates and keeps Compiler and virtualization analyses
separate.

Each candidate's dossier owns its verdict, rejected alternatives, source
identity and reopening conditions. Neither iteration supplies an aggregate
architecture winner or proof of production adoption. Budget failures remain
failures; incomparable hosts and cohorts remain incomparable.

The September 10 execution records five retained local changes and three
no-adoption outcomes from eight Pursue dossiers. Its follow-through rejects six
additional interventions and retains four absolute budget gaps. Reuse those
accounts with their original source and binding limits. The recovered execution
records remain historical and unbound.

Later static-preview and content-root proof keeps its original scopes. A
no-regression claim does not inherit a parser speedup requirement. The latest
content-root matrix retains six complete latency/text cells and two AI-rich
cells with inconclusive profile attribution. Distinct 1.20 and 1.25 profile
policies remain distinct evidence.

Use `node tooling/scripts/review-ledger.mjs research <key-or-term>` to locate
earlier lead/read/rejection rows. Read the corresponding candidate and receipt
before spending another investigation on it. A new source or proof family may
justify reopening; a repeated review request also permits fresh reasoning
without another broad external crawl.

[The design plan](../../plans/2026-09-30-performance-evidence-design.md) specifies
one target authority, a latest receipt of what ran for every target outcome,
admission at the existing loader and one active S5 evaluator with separate
frozen work-gain and no-regression policies. Its five execution slices cover
every consumer and preserve the original receipts and archived verdicts.
Admission compares the inputs each producer records, labelled by what it
compared, plus served-build identity for browser producers. A producer that
records nothing stays unknown. S3 runs the functional contract on the matrix
fixtures against both served builds before the matrix and records reference
hashes the evaluator requires. Equal arm hashes cannot replace that reference.

The [design outcome](../review-records/2026-09-30-performance-evidence-design.json)
closes planning with partial proof. Source checks and a bounded draft review
establish the design; implementation and native performance evidence remain
unexecuted. Concurrent shared-doctrine edits were reconciled in the design
record without rewriting the original audit's fingerprints.

The [cuts review](../review-records/2026-09-30-performance-evidence-cuts.json)
removed machinery no reproduced defect required: the immutable attempt history,
two executable policies kept only to regrade archived matrices, the complete
loaded-source protocol and reference production inside the timed producer. The
[amended design outcome](../review-records/2026-09-30-performance-evidence-design-cuts.json)
also drops the declared target environment map. Recipes keep their inline
assignments, the receipt records each recipe verbatim, and the runner removes
every recipe-named variable from the inherited environment. One correctness
recipe smoke-runs its producer with values its benchmark must not inherit, so
one shared environment would break it. A variable that a producer reads but no
recipe names can still come from the caller's shell.

The S1 checkpoints ([first](../review-records/2026-09-30-performance-evidence-s1.json),
[repairs](../review-records/2026-09-30-performance-evidence-s1-repairs.json))
adopt one recipe per command, recipe-named environment isolation and a latest
receipt for every target run, including failures. The
[execution outcome](../review-records/2026-09-30-performance-evidence-execution.json)
adopts the rest of the design. Target artifacts carry the lab's interpretation
metadata, and the lab admits each artifact once as current, stale or unknown
from its receipt, its recipe and the inputs its producer recorded. Only current
rows read `ok` or count as coverage. One S5 evaluator assesses matrices under the
no-regression and 1.20 work-gain policies with a per-cell preflight bound to
each served build. The code-block clock starts before the editor's own
handlers, external-text samples are dense and copied, and seven strict
producers write their measurements before validating.

The [repairs outcome](../review-records/2026-09-30-performance-evidence-repairs.json)
closes what the same-family trail review found. Both arms must share one
preflight reference, so a candidate that renders different text everywhere
fails correctness; on real builds the evaluator failed such a candidate and
passed it without that rule. A failed latest run shows its failure stage
instead of older values, threshold rows use the comparison operator their
producer records, and admission reads recorded inputs only from
`sourceIdentity.measuredInputs`. The `core-node-transforms` guard exposed a
Plite regression. `tx.nodes.wrap` with a root path and a selector changed
nothing. With the binding fixed, that target and `collab-readiness` reran and
admit `current`.

Seventeen of the twenty lab artifacts come from producers that record no
inputs, so they stay unknown even after a rerun. The cuts review expected a
loaded-source census to leave most rows unknown; admission did the same until
targets reran, so that argument did not separate the two designs. The native
code-block renderer exceeded its absolute input and navigation budgets in two
shared-host runs; without a matched baseline that is inconclusive, and the
navigation metric does not depend on the input clock. The 1.20 attribution
guard sits within the noise of identical builds (1.1991). These are owner
findings; the execution plan names each owner and destination.

[The ledger](../reviews.md#performance) records review order and source entry
points. [The execution plan](../../plans/2026-09-30-performance-evidence-execution.md)
records the adoption and its open findings. Benchmark keeps its receipt, paired
comparison and adoption laws. Design does not establish implementation or
runtime proof. The unbound September 7 research plan remains historical context;
neither its completion nor its recovery record supplies current adoption.
