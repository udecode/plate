<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-13-editor-width-padding-variants","kind":"fix","migrationNotes":[]} -->

- **`editor`**, **`editor-static`**: Size the side padding of the `default`, `demo` and `fullWidth` variants to the editor's own width instead of the viewport. Padding shrinks to 24 px in a narrow editor, so a 180 px editor gets a 132 px text column instead of 52 px. Editors at least 512 px wide, or 640 px for `fullWidth`, keep their current padding.

<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-13-editor-width-padding-demos","kind":"fix","migrationNotes":[]} -->

- **`comment-demo`**, **`comment-overlap-demo`**, **`comment-persistence-demo`**, **`comment-review-demo`**, **`suggestion-demo`**, **`suggestion-format-demo`**, **`suggestion-persistence-demo`**, **`suggestion-view-demo`**: Size the editor's side padding to its own width instead of the viewport. Padding shrinks to 24 px in a narrow editor, and editors at least 600 px wide keep 48 px.
