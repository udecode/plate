- **`board`**: Add `BoardKit`, a whiteboard block with notes, geo shapes, arrows, frames and pen lines. Add it beside `EditorKit`. Its toolbar shows an icon for the select, note, arrow, frame, pen and eraser tools, a shape menu of rectangle, ellipse, diamond and triangle, a fill menu of colors, zoom out, the zoom level as a percentage, zoom in and a button that fits every shape in view. A click on the zoom level goes back to 100%. The eraser fades every note, shape, arrow and pen line a click or drag passes over to 30% and removes them when you let go, and never takes a frame. A shape with no fill and no label goes only where the eraser passes near its outline, so a pen line drawn inside an empty rectangle goes alone. With a frame selected, an icon removes the frame and keeps its shapes. Picking a color fills the selected notes and geo shapes. With none selected, it fills the next note or geo shape you add. The copied board writes every word the board says in `boardMessages`, and the board tells screen readers the selected shape and its place, the picked tool, the zoom and how many shapes a removal took off. The board is one Tab stop. When it takes focus, screen readers read the keys in `boardMessages.instructions`, and the copied board draws a focus ring while no shape is selected. Command with an arrow on a Mac, or Control with an arrow elsewhere, selects the nearest shape in that direction. `=`, `+` and `-` zoom by steps, Shift+0 resets the zoom, Shift+1 fits every shape, and PageUp and PageDown pan, sideways with Shift. With the note, shape or frame tool, Enter places that shape. With the arrow tool and one shape that is not an arrow selected, Enter starts an arrow from it, and Tab, or Command or Control with an arrow, aims at the other shape. Enter then draws the arrow. `boardMessages.connecting` and `boardMessages.aim` say each step. Shift+Enter moves focus from the board to its toolbar, and Escape moves it back. A Yjs room created before the board refuses an editor with it, so seed a new room from the old room's document, as the Board docs page shows.
