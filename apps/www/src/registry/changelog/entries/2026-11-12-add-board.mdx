- **`board`**: Add `BoardKit`, a whiteboard block with notes, geo shapes, arrows, frames and pen lines, and its toolbar of select, note, shape, arrow, frame and pen tools. Add it beside `EditorKit`. The copied board writes every word the board says in `boardMessages`, and the board tells screen readers the selected shape and its place, the picked tool and how many shapes a removal took off. A Yjs room created before the board refuses an editor with it, so seed a new room from the old room's document, as the Board docs page shows.
