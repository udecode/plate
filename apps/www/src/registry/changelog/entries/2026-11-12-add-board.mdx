- **`board`**: Add `BoardKit`, a whiteboard block with notes, geo shapes, arrows, frames and pen lines. Add it beside `EditorKit`. Its toolbar shows an icon for the select, hand, note, arrow, frame, pen and eraser tools, a shape menu of rectangle, ellipse, diamond and triangle, a lock button that keeps the drawing tool picked, zoom out, the zoom level as a percentage, zoom in and a button that fits every shape in view. A click on the zoom level goes back to 100%. A drag with the hand pans the board from any point, over shapes too, and shows an open hand. The eraser fades every note, shape, arrow and pen line a click or drag passes over to 30% and removes them when you let go, and never takes a frame. A shape with no fill and no label goes only where the eraser passes near its outline, so a pen line drawn inside an empty rectangle goes alone. With a frame selected, an icon removes the frame and keeps its shapes. `BoardStylePanel` docks at the board's top right with a row for each style the selected shapes take: shape, fill, outline color, line style, outline width and text size. A pick restyles the selected shapes and the shape you are typing in as one undo step. With a drawing tool picked, it styles the next shape. On a board narrower than 36rem the panel folds behind a Styles button. A pick keeps the caret, except in that folded panel, whose button moves focus into it. The copied board writes every word the board says in `boardMessages`, and the board tells screen readers the selected shape and its place, the picked tool, the zoom and how many shapes a removal took off. The board is one Tab stop. When it takes focus, screen readers read the keys in `boardMessages.instructions`, and the copied board draws a focus ring while no shape is selected. Command with an arrow on a Mac, or Control with an arrow elsewhere, selects the nearest shape in that direction. `=`, `+` and `-` zoom by steps, Shift+0 resets the zoom, Shift+1 fits every shape, H picks the hand, and PageUp and PageDown pan, sideways with Shift. With the note, shape or frame tool, Enter places that shape. Q or the toolbar's lock button turns on a tool lock that keeps the note, shape, frame or arrow tool picked after each shape it adds, and a second press turns it off. `boardMessages.toolLock` says each change. Command+D on a Mac, or Control+D elsewhere, copies the selected shapes 24px down and right, or further when another shape sits there, and selects the copies. With the arrow tool and one shape that is not an arrow selected, Enter starts an arrow from it, and Tab, or Command or Control with an arrow, aims at the other shape. The arrow keys then move the arrow's end, which binds to the shape under it other than the one its start binds to, to a frame only on its edge and to a pen line only on its line, and otherwise stays free. Enter then draws the arrow. With the arrow tool and nothing selected, Enter starts an arrow at the view's center. Its start binds to the shape under it that is not an arrow, to a frame only on its edge and to a pen line only on its line, and otherwise stays free, there and after each arrow key moves it. Tab, or Command or Control with an arrow, aims it at a shape. A second Enter keeps the start, and the end then moves the same way. `boardMessages.starting`, `boardMessages.connecting`, `boardMessages.aim` and `boardMessages.free` say each step, and `starting` names the shape the start binds to, or its point when it sits free. With one shape selected that is not an arrow, S resizes it from its bottom-right corner. Tab picks another corner, the arrow keys move the corner, Enter keeps the new size and Escape puts it back. `boardMessages.resizing` and `boardMessages.resize` name the corner and the size at each step. Space starts picking shapes: Tab, or Command or Control with an arrow, moves a dashed ring from shape to shape without changing the selection, Space adds the ringed shape to the selection or takes it out, and Enter or Escape keeps the selection. `boardMessages.picking` and `boardMessages.pick` say each step. Shift+Enter moves focus from the board to its toolbar, and Escape moves it back. A Yjs room created before the board refuses an editor with it, so seed a new room from the old room's document, as the Board docs page shows.
