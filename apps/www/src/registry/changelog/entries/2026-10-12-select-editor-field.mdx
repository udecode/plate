<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-12-select-editor-rebuild-one-field-bd511354","kind":"behavior","migrationNotes":["Replace SelectEditorContent, SelectEditorInput and SelectEditorCombobox with one SelectEditor that takes items, value or defaultValue, onValueChange and the editable's props."]} -->

- **`select-editor`**: Replace `SelectEditorContent`, `SelectEditorInput`, `SelectEditorCombobox` and `select-command.tsx` with one `SelectEditor`. It keeps the chips as the only value, writes the form's `value` back whenever it differs from the chips, and passes `ref`, `onBlur` and ARIA props to the editable.

<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-12-select-editor-keys-links-7c2e41a9","kind":"fix","migrationNotes":[]} -->

- **`select-editor`**: Move the active option with ArrowUp and ArrowDown wherever the caret is, close the menu and clear the search on Escape and mod+Enter, and link a read-only chip to its `url` only when `getLinkHref` accepts it, so a chip with a script or malformed `url` shows without a link instead of breaking the field.

<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-12-select-editor-tag-item-5d90b3e2","kind":"remove","migrationNotes":["Install select-editor instead of tag; it copies tag.tsx with the tag element plugin."]} -->

- **`select-editor`**: Ship `tag.tsx`, with the `tag` element plugin and its chip, in this item, and remove the separate `tag` item and the Radix dependencies.
