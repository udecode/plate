- **`suggestion`**: Paint every part of one change in one color, and each change in its own color, even when one person made them all.

<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-13-suggestion-block-marks","kind":"new","migrationNotes":[]} -->

- **`suggestion`**: Mark a suggested line break, a removed line break, or a whole-block change such as a paragraph turned into a heading, in the left margin in its change's color.

<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-13-suggestion-refusals","kind":"behavior","migrationNotes":[]} -->

- **`suggestion`**, **`mode-toolbar-button`**: Show a toast when Suggesting refuses a change that touches a code block or a table, or a change to a list item itself such as Enter, and leave the document as it was. Text typed inside a list item shows as a suggestion. The mode menu switches to Suggesting only when the switch takes.

<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-13-discussion-card-decide","kind":"behavior","migrationNotes":[]} -->

- **`discussion`**: Accept and Reject on a suggestion card decide the change that card shows.
