- **`comment`**, **`discussion`**: Await comment actions before clearing input, retain failed replies for retry, reopen resolved conversations independently of document undo, and keep suggestion conversations replyable after decisions.
- **`use-chat`**: Let completed AI comments finish saving when the chat menu closes; discard pending drafts on cancellation.
- **`comment-persistence-demo`**, **`suggestion-persistence-demo`**, **`editor-ai`**: Save comments and opaque attachments with the matching document revision and restore them into a fresh editor.
- **`discussion-demo`**: Load three authored suggestions and their conversations together while keeping direct editing as the default.
