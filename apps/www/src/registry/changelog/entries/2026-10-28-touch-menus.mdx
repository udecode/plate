<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-28-block-menu-keep-touch-selection-editors-ed1b993a","kind":"fix","migrationNotes":[]} -->

- **`block-menu`**: On a touch screen, a long press or a double tap in an editor with the block menu now keeps the browser's own word selection and its Copy menu. Before, a long press turned the paragraph into a block selection, and Paste then replaced the whole paragraph.
<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-28-editor-dropdown-menu-open-on-tap","kind":"fix","migrationNotes":[]} -->
- **`editor-dropdown-menu`**: On a touch screen, a dropdown menu now opens on a tap, so a swipe that scrolls the toolbar no longer opens the menu of the button it starts on. A mouse press still opens the menu on press.
<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-28-toolbar-touch-swipe-closes","kind":"fix","migrationNotes":[]} -->
- **`toolbar`**, **`toolbar-overlay`**: On a touch screen, a swipe that starts on a floating toolbar menu button no longer keeps the floating toolbar open after the editor loses its selection.
<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-10-28-toolbar-overlay-trigger-handlers","kind":"behavior","migrationNotes":["Call `opening()` where you called the function `useToolbarOverlayTrigger` returned, and call `cancelled()` from the trigger's `onPointerCancel`."]} -->
- **`toolbar-overlay`**: `useToolbarOverlayTrigger` now returns `{ opening, cancelled }` instead of one function. Call `opening()` where you called that function, and call `cancelled()` from the trigger's `onPointerCancel`.
