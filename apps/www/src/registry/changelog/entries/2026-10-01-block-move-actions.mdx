- **`dnd`**: Clicking a block's drag handle opens Move up, Move down and Cut, and `Mod+Shift+ArrowUp` / `Mod+Shift+ArrowDown` move the blocks around the caret. Dropping a block into another editor copies it, and Alt (Apple) or Ctrl copies inside one editor.
- **`column`**: Clicking a column's drag handle opens Move left and Move right.
- **`table`**: Clicking a row's drag handle opens Select row, Move up and Move down; the handle's label is "Drag row or open row actions".
- **`block-menu`**: The right-click menu offers Move up, Move down and Cut.
