- **`field`**: The Kind select offers Multiple choice, with an Options list and Min and Max inputs that save on blur, Enter, or close. A count the settings refuse keeps the settings open with its text and error. The signer picks options from a checkbox list that says how many to pick and disables unticked options once the field holds `max` of them. Unticking the last option clears the answer.
