- **`autoformat`**: Remove the `~0`–`~9`, `^0`–`^9`, `~+`, `~-`, `^+` and `^-` unicode substitutions, so typed `~2~` and `^2^` become subscript and superscript with `basic-marks` instead of `₂~` and `²^`. `^o` still becomes `°`.
