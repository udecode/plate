- **`floating-toolbar`**, **`link`**, **`ai`**: The floating toolbar, the link toolbar and the AI menu render in the new `caret` slot, so they follow the caret into a board note inside the page and act on the note. A toolbar of another `EditorContent`, such as a header, stays on its own content while the caret is in the body or its note. In a note, the floating toolbar now shows the same buttons as on the page.
