- **`link`**: Remove the `forceSubmit` state. The form saves a new or changed address that starts with `http://` or `https://`, has another scheme such as `mailto:`, or starts with `/`, `./`, `../`, `#` or `?`. It stays open on any other draft, such as `www.example.com`, and when `allowedSchemes` refuses the address. Editing only the link text keeps the stored address.
