- **`lint`**: Add `LintKit`, which configures `BaseLintPlugin` with two example rules that skip code blocks, wavy underlines by severity, and `F8` and `Shift+F8` to select the next and previous finding. `LintPopover` shows the finding under the caret with its fixes and Fix and next. `useLintFindings` and `useLintFindingAt` read the findings for your own UI.
- **`lint-demo`**: Add a lint example with a findings panel that runs, clears and applies the first fix, and a Check with server button backed by a stand-in rule that answers after 600 ms.
