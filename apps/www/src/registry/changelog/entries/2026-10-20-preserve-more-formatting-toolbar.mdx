- **`more-toolbar-button`**: Keep the floating toolbar and the selected text when a second click on More formatting, or Escape, closes it.
- **`editor-dropdown-menu`**: When a click outside a non-modal menu, a right-click outside any menu, Tab, or a focus move to another control closes a toolbar menu, focus stays where the user put it instead of jumping back into the editor. This covers More formatting, Turn into, font color and the table menus. A press on another button in the menu's own toolbar still puts focus back in the editor. Base UI menus close without an exit animation, as Radix menus already do, so a click right after a close keeps its focus.
