- **`caption`**, **`caption-static`**, **`resize-handle`**: Place media aligned `start` or `end` on the text's start or end side, and keep media aligned `left` or `right` on that side in any direction
- **`media-embed-static`**, **`media-image-static`**: Center an unaligned image or embed in static rendering, as the editor does
- **`media-file`**, **`media-file-static`**: Align a file's caption to the text's start side
- **`resize-handle`**: Keep each resize handle on its own side in right-to-left text, with arrow keys that follow the text direction
