- **`format-painter`**, **`editor-plugins`**: Add `FormatPainterKit`, which installs the format painter with `Mod+Alt+C` to copy a look, `Mod+Alt+V` to paste it and `Escape` to put the brush down, and include it in the editor kit.
<!-- entry: {"id":"2026-11-17-format-painter-toolbar-button","kind":"new","migrationNotes":[]} -->
- **`format-painter-toolbar-button`**, **`fixed-toolbar`**: Add a paintbrush button to the fixed toolbar, next to the clear formatting button. A click paints once, a double click keeps painting until `Escape`, and a click on the armed button puts it down.
