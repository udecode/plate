- **`footnote`**: In a read-only view, a click or Enter on a footnote mark scrolls to its note and flashes it, and the note's back link jumps back to the mark, with focus on the control you land on. A back link of a note cited several times opens its picker with focus inside it. An unresolved mark does nothing in a read-only view.
- **`footnote-static`**: Link each mark to its note and each note back to its first mark, with `fnref-` and `fn-` ids, so static pages and exported HTML keep working footnote links.
