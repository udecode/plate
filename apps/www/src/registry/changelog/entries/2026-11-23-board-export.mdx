- **`board`**: Add an Export menu to the board toolbar that saves the board, or its selected shapes, as `board.png` or `board.svg`, or copies it to the clipboard as a PNG or as SVG text. Add `board-static.tsx`, whose `BoardPictureKit` draws each shape in the picture. The menu shows its notices for images, fonts and shapes the picture could not draw, and for an empty board, through `sonner`. When you update a copied board, copy `board-static.tsx` and the static items it imports, add the `sonner` dependency, and mount one `<Toaster />` in your app.
- **`block-list-static`**: Draw a task's checkbox with your registry style's checkbox classes, so a picture or static render of a task list draws the checkbox the editor draws.
- **`html-export`**: Copy your page's body classes onto the exported document, so a style your CSS scopes under a body class draws in the preview and the saved file. Keep the document's doctype, so the saved file renders in standards mode.
