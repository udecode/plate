Source: [Lauren Tan’s pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/93b00b89ef425a9c1bac0d0b317dfc49c930ac99/pstack/skills/technical-writing). Copyright 2026 Lauren Tan; MIT license in LICENSE.

The four writing layers and worked example are retained with explicit adaptations:
house style takes precedence, document modes guide sections without forcing page
splits, and mechanical grammar rules become clarity checks. Preservation,
author voice, prose-audit modes, a pattern catalog and a deterministic scanner
are integrated from Dotai's Unslop synthesis. There is one writing entrypoint.
Dotai also owns general example selection: clarify reader tasks, claims and
likely misunderstandings while preserving document modes, existing examples,
scope and evidence limits. Project owners choose page presentation and domain
coverage.

[UPSTREAM.json](UPSTREAM.json) records the pinned upstream file, its original
and current SHA-256, exact adaptation diff, reasons and owned supporting files.
[Anti-slop source notes](references/anti-slop-sources.md) retain the synthesis's
original source/license accounting. Plain writing does not require a separate
engineering runtime adapter; current user and repository instructions govern
tools, source edits and publication.

Refresh from the pinned source, review every changed instruction, update the per-file provenance, and run `python3 scripts/check-pstack-preservation.py` from the Dotai root before installation. Do not replace a full method with a summary.
