# Task

Handle $ARGUMENTS. Keep one lifecycle and the user's entire intended outcome.
Read [the Plate workflow](./references/workflow.md) first. Read the user-named
source and nearby owners before selecting a method. Proceed through already
authorized work; an internal handoff is not a reason to stop.

## Engineering method

Use the full [Poteto Mode](../poteto-mode/SKILL.md), its Codex adapter and the
matched playbook for substantive engineering work. Read the relevant principle
leaves, examples and worker prompts. For an unmatched complex effort use
[Figure It Out](../figure-it-out/SKILL.md). These supply methods within Task.

Choose methods for a concrete question: How traces source flow; Why recovers
rationale; Architect and Arena explore competing structures; Blast Radius
tests consequential assumptions; Interrogate challenges a contested decision.
Do not run a fixed chain of every method. Preserve full upstream instructions
when one applies and disclose unavailable tool or model-family independence.

For a question or tiny local edit, answer or make the edit and verify it
directly. Do not manufacture a goal, plan, panel, app launch or new test.

For GitHub sources, route public issue/PR/security work to Maintainer. For a
Linear item, fetch the issue, comments and relevant attachments through its
connector. The latest user request governs scope; a tracker proposal is
evidence to challenge. Video Transcripts owns available recording evidence.
If a recording is unavailable, continue independent source work and name what
could not be observed.

Route from the actual owner and claim. A public issue's diagnosis is evidence
to challenge, not an instruction to copy. A normalized local case may retain
public provenance without granting public mutation. Plate product policy stays
out of Plite substrate. Load the complete chosen specialist method.

## Plan, execute, prove

For work needing durable state, record outcome, bounded scope, acceptance,
decisions, risks, proof and next action in one file. Use the task template or
the relevant full project template when its case matrices help. Complex and
closure templates are `task-complex` and `task-closure`. The user's standing
request includes Autogoal for every long-running workflow unless they opt out. Apply
[Autogoal's criteria](../autogoal/SKILL.md), including large audits and multi-gap
implementation, before extended work or when a small task grows. No separate
invocation or confirmation is needed; quick questions and small edits do not
qualify. Persist every applicable source-linked checklist obligation before long
work and reconcile the original checklists before closure under Autogoal. Short
work checks those obligations directly. Carry the same goal, plan and review
budget across workers.

Implement in verifiable units. Reproduce a claimed defect on its actual
surface, fix the durable owner, and run the narrowest affected proof. A new
public contract uses Best API; a measured hot path uses Benchmark. Keep real
native behavior, serialized-data and package laws intact during hard cuts.

Verify Plate owns proof selection and evidence. Task owns authorized repairs.
Run the existing applicable checks; do not add tests or broaden the runner just
because another skill lists possible cases. An observed costly regression may
justify the smallest public-boundary test under Testing's policy.

For a long or unattended run, use Show Me Your Work's append-only decision
trail and link existing receipts. Resolve failed evidence before advancing.
Claim exact local, integrated and released states separately.

## Closeout

Inspect the final result and report outcome, reason, proof and material limits.
The Plate workflow owns the single explicit-review/PR-closeout budget and the
ban on Autoreview on `next`. Do not add another closure panel or two-clean-pass
ritual. Relevant package, registry, docs, doctrine and release safeguards stay
with their owners. Publication requires actual authority and final read-back.
