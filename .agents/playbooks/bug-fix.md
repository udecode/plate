# Bug fix

- **In** "Reproduce it yourself on the matching surface via the driver skill": attached screenshots and recordings are the report; transcribe a recording with `video-transcripts` and keep the reporter's exact route, input and expected result. "Regression vs main" means reproducing on `main` in a detached worktree as the baseline, then on the current checkout, and naming the change that broke it.
- **In** "Binary-search the cause": tighten the loop first, to seconds rather than minutes, asserting the specific symptom, with time pinned and randomness seeded; raise a flaky bug's reproduction rate until it can be debugged. Each hypothesis states a falsifiable prediction ("if X is the cause, changing Y makes the bug disappear"), and each probe tests one prediction by changing one variable at a time with targeted boundary logs, never log-everything.
- **After** "Binary-search the cause": `diagnose` stops here with the cause and its evidence.
- **In** "Plan the fix": fix at the owning layer. Plite owns substrate behavior, and Plate owns product and plugin policy. Repair the violated invariant and what let it escape, per Pokayoke in `AGENTS.md`, never with a caller workaround.
- **After** "Verify on the same surface": a fix already reported as done, locally or publicly, that then fails its exact replay or final verification, or that the reporter contradicts, is a failed fix; the expected red before a fix is not one. The reporter assertion must run before a failure counts: a proof host that crashes or fails before the assertion is repaired at its own owner, and the same product attempt restarts. Before another product attempt: revoke the earlier green and completion claims, leaving public corrections to the authorized public coordinator. Freeze the product bytes, rerun the failing assertion and name what made it red: product nondeterminism, interaction delivery, host readiness or oracle sampling; repeated passes without that answer do not resume product edits. For native selection, focus, popup or toolbar focus, mounted keyboard or semantic commands, or an exact route, apply `verify`'s [failed native diagnostics](../rules/verify/references/regression-oracles.md#failed-native-diagnostics) first. Name why the earlier proof passed (the product invariant, test boundary, runner, served source or completion decision) and repair that gap at its owner, preferring an assertion that rejects the escaped case over new instructions. When an existing gate already rejects the failed packet, record no change with that gate's passing evidence; never reword an enforced instruction just to produce a diff, and a defer cannot close a required gap. Then restart from exact reproduction with every still-applicable acceptance item; an outcome inferred during the failed attempt does not carry forward. On the second failed fix, or at once when the fix compensates across layers, duplicates a live identity, adds per-node hot work, depends on a timer for focus correctness or repairs substrate state from UI, run `best-api-review` on the owner before another attempt; repair authority continues through that review. The review compares deleting or merging the implicated owners before adding compensation, and its Stop may keep the existing owner with a stated reason.
- **After** "Verify on the same surface": tag every debug probe with a unique prefix, then grep-remove them all and delete throwaway harnesses before done. A published package edit requires its applicable changeset per `changeset`.
- **Replace** "Stage the commits so the failing repro lands before the fix": a test written first lands together with the fix, never as a failing commit of its own, per `AGENTS.md`'s Tests rule; the owner commits, per the Delivery rule.
- **Replace** "Run **Opening a PR**": open a PR only when the user asks, per the Delivery rule.
- Explicit corpus, harness, rewrite-closure or repeated regression work loads `verify`'s corpus mode with its plan template, validator and proof receipts.
- A public GitHub issue or PR is triaged by `maintainer` first; once `maintainer` routes a confirmed local bug here, this playbook fixes it.
