# API review

The first two stops of the v2 loop pick the next ledger item and judge it. `best-api-review` holds the method and records the verdict; this playbook orders the work and says where to stop.

- **Before** "Route through the **how** skill": pick the item. For "next", or `best-api-review` with no target, run `node tooling/scripts/review-ledger.mjs next` and `node tooling/scripts/review-ledger.mjs status`. Answer on the unit's page, per `AGENTS.md`'s Plan pages: write `docs/plans/<date>-<scope>-review.md` with the scope first in `review_scopes`, `Status: proposed: waiting on review` and `Playbook: api-review`. Its lead paragraph names the unit and why it is open, Evidence holds the prior-work summary and the release counts, and Open questions recommends one move for the unit's state, with another scope and hold as the other options: review for an unreviewed scope or a stale verdict, plan for a Pursue verdict with no bound adoption. "go" takes the recommendation. The pick stays the owner's. The page asks it under Open questions even though it recommends one move, and for this pick this playbook overrides Plan pages' rule that a call with a safe default becomes a Defaults row. When the scope has no subject file, create `docs/plans/topics/<scope>.md` with its title, its ledger question as the lead and its current owners under `## Main changes`. Publish the page, reply with its link, then stop. An unreviewed scope needs a review. A `fork` scope has two current reviews and needs a review that reconciles both. A `pursue-unbound` scope has a landed plan naming its Pursue with no execution recorded, so record that execution through `draft <plan.md>` and `record`, and plan what is still missing. A `pursue-not-adopted` scope has no completed execution standing for its Pursue and needs a plan. `next` skips deferred scopes, which wait on the evidence their verdict names; `status` still counts them. To take a different item, name its scope. When the reviewed scope is not the one `next` returned, say so on the page and in the record's `trigger`, and name any open scope it `dependsOn`.
- **Replace** "Produce the `how`-shaped output": for a named scope, run `best-api-review` on it. Write the verdict into the scope's iteration, the file "next" wrote or a new `docs/plans/<date>-<scope>-<slug>.md`, before any panel, so the panel's rows land beside it. Its lead paragraph gives the verdict and the strongest reason, `## Public API` pairs the current and proposed normal call sites, Defaults hold the picks among the lanes, Evidence holds the lanes considered and the proof limits, and, for a Stop or Defer verdict, Open questions offers again, interrogate or hold. `Status:` becomes `planning` for Pursue, `closed: Stop verdict` for Stop, or `blocked: waiting on <evidence>` for Defer. Give the subject file real current call sites under `## Public API` when it lacks them. Write and `record` the review as that skill says, and update the decision page's text when the verdict changes its conclusion. A Stop or Defer verdict then publishes the page, replies with its link and stops. A Pursue verdict continues in the same run into the Plan playbook on the same file, because the owner decides whether to pursue at the plan's Build now or Hold. A refused or blocked record follows that skill's Never leave a record pending.
- **Replace** "Apply the **unslop** skill to the reply": write the reply clean as drafted, with no separate pass, per `AGENTS.md`'s Writing passes rule. The review record, the decision page and the page's plan get one `unslop` pass.
- After a Stop or Defer verdict, the answer to the page's question, or a typed word, picks the next step. "again" runs `best-api-review` once more on the same scope and challenges the strongest alternative afresh; its record reaffirms or supersedes the last one. "interrogate" or "panel" runs the panel on the verdict's target with the configured seats. "plan" opens the Plan playbook on the verdict's file anyway. "next" starts over at the first stop.
