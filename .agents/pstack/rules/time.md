# Time budget

This file says how long a command, proof, seat or wait may run, and where a measurement that cannot fit goes. The pstack block in `AGENTS.md` points here. The `sync-pstack` skill renders this file from its shared source in `udecode/dotai`, so change it there.

- **Time budget.** No command, proof or seat that a run starts takes more than an hour, so development stays fast (owner's word, 2026-10-16); only a stress run, below, takes longer. No wait on the machine's state, such as a quiet host, lasts longer either. When such a wait reaches its hour, the lead logs the line it guards `inconclusive` with `proof:` its last log, names its next probe and goes on. The budget keeps a lead from holding long work while other chips wait to land, so long work runs in a dedicated chip. Before a proof that may run long, the lead times one sample and narrows the proof to fit. It never runs one protocol as pieces that together pass the hour. A measurement that a decision waits on, such as a probe before a design is accepted, narrows to fit as well, and an inconclusive narrowed result leaves that decision open with its next probe named. Any other measurement that cannot fit, such as a soak, a large-cohort sweep, a multi-round interleaved timing protocol or a final rerun of built code, never blocks the run or its close. When it is still worth running, the lead offers it as a suggested task through the `chips` skill's Offer mode, waiting `run:<the run's plan>`, so its card's worktree starts from the landed change. The offer names the command, the source identity it must measure, its budget, what a miss triggers and the plan step it settles. The measurement goes to `## Open work` with `owner:` the owner and `stop:` that task landing or being dropped, and this hand-off is the owner's word that the narrower exit condition counts as done. The Close claims only what the run measured. The card's session confirms that its worktree holds that source identity before it runs, and stops and tells the owner when it does not. A conclusive miss it finds is a pivot finding, which that session leads as the Autopilot rule says. Only that session, or the run once the owner asks, passes `--stress`. Without `--stress`, `proof.mjs` stops a command after 3,600 seconds or its shorter `--timeout`, kills every process still in the command's process group and logs the run as timed out. `proof.mjs` and `cross.mjs` refuse a longer `--timeout` without `--stress`.
