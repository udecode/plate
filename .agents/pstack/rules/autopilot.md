# Autopilot

This file says which calls the lead makes on its own, which stops wait on the owner, and when a finding pivots a run. The pstack block in `AGENTS.md` points here. Edit its shared source in `udecode/ustack`.

- **Autopilot.** Work runs to completion without pausing for the owner. Classify noticeable calls by attention level (`safe`, `look`, `answer`), take the recommended pick (or most reversible pick if unclear), record it in `## Defaults` (or the reply if planless) with the word that reverses it, and keep going. Minor calls need at most a log row.
  - **Hard stops.** Stop under `## Open questions` only for: a production deploy or release, pushing `main`, force-pushing a shared branch, deleting non-run data (excluding safe items `cleanup` handles), a missing product sign-in needed during exploration, or a configured models-sheet model that cannot run (except seats `panel.md` skips).
  - **Third plan stop.** Also stop before starting a third plan on one topic, as `long-runs.md` says.
  - **Not stops.** Drafted external messages (held per `messages.md`), pending external gates (staging, quota reset, running seats), and owner status questions are not stops; keep doing ready work or schedule a wakeup. Prototypes, arenas, and `architect` pick a winner and continue unless the request is plan-only, prototype-only, or carries "stop at plan", "stop at design", or "stop before ship".
  - **Pivots.** If a proven finding shows the accepted design cannot meet a requirement (such as a conclusive budget miss other than a `time.md` timing miss, or a stored-shape limit no hillclimb iteration can fix), pivot immediately: run `architect` (or a direct design pass under "fast"), write the replacement plan, and build it next. Never defer a design-breaking finding to Open work unless the owner explicitly asked to hold it until a date.
