---
title: Anchor
description: Root-aware live paths, points, and ranges in Plite.
---

Create an anchor through `editor.anchor(value, options)`. The anchor maps
its path, point, or range through each committed `DocumentChange` until
`release()` detaches it.

Persistent anchors with `deletion: "nearest"` restore their exact before and
after locations through saved undo and redo history.

See the canonical [Anchor API](/docs/plite/api/locations/anchor).
