'plitejs': patch

- Fix undoing a merge of a line in one quote into a line of the quote before it, or into a paragraph before its quote, after a teammate's edit. A letter the teammate typed in the moved letters was lost, and after the teammate wrapped the merged line in a quote, the undo lost the second line. The undo now keeps the teammate's letter and puts the second line back in its quote, and a redo keeps a letter the teammate typed in the line the undo brought back. This also holds when one Yjs update from the teammate wraps the merged line twice, or changes one of its properties other than its type and wraps it. When the line merged into a paragraph and the teammate then wrapped that paragraph in a quote, the undo keeps the quote around that paragraph and puts the second line back in its own quote after it. Some cases still lose letters. When the line merged into a paragraph and the teammate typed in that paragraph before wrapping it, or wrapped it in two quotes, the undo still loses the second line.

- When a Yjs teammate wraps a top-level paragraph or quote, the import now keeps that block's letters instead of replacing the whole block. So when the teammate wrapped the quote that holds the merged line, once, twice, or after changing the line's alignment, the undo puts the second line back in its own quote with its moved letters, and a caret in the wrapped block stays on its text.
