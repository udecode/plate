- Fix undoing a merge of a line in one quote into a line of the quote before it, or into a paragraph before its quote, after a teammate's edit. A letter the teammate typed in the moved letters was lost, and after the teammate wrapped the merged line in a quote, the undo lost the second line. The undo now keeps the teammate's letter and puts the second line back in its quote, and a redo keeps a letter the teammate typed in the line the undo brought back. When the line merged into a paragraph and the teammate then wrapped that paragraph in a quote, the undo keeps the quote around that paragraph and puts the second line back in its own quote after it. Some cases still lose letters. When one Yjs update from the teammate wraps the merged line twice, or wraps it and also changes its properties, the undo can put the moved letters on the wrong line. When the line merged into a paragraph and the teammate typed in that paragraph before wrapping it, or wrapped it in two quotes, the undo still loses the second line.
