Make `useRootEditor()` and `useCaretEditor()` return one editor per root and `readOnly` option under an `EditorRoot`, so a lock set through that editor holds after the caret leaves its root and comes back. UI outside every editing area gets one editor for a root. A renderer inside an editing area gets another editor for that root, so a lock set through the editor outside never reaches a renderer, such as a shape on a board. While no editing area shows the root, `useCommand()` for that root runs on the same editor, so that lock refuses its commands, also when the `EditorRoot` mounts no editing area. The editor's `isFocused()` and `isComposing()` report the editing area that shows its root, and its `focus()` and `blur()` set that editing area's focus. They throw while no editing area shows the root. Under an `EditorRoot` that mounts no editing area, the editor of the root that holds the caret follows the editing area that holds the caret in another `EditorRoot`, so its `api.dom` calls reach that area. A reader of the editor sees an `EditorRoot` lock or unlock in the render that changes it. The reader also sees a lock before the render that sets it returns when that render moves the caret to another copy of the root, or mounts or remounts the editing area that shows the root. A listener of the editor hears the focus of an editing area that mounts with `autoFocus` in the same turn as that area's own listeners. One change still reaches the reader one microtask later: the read-only state of an editing area that a render mounts or unmounts without a lock or focus change.
