Store what a suggested Enter gives its new line in a `gave` record on the split's review mark, such as the paragraph type a title's slot gives it, a default its place fills in, or the removal of a pending value it cannot take. Documents saved before this change still load. A build from before this change refuses a document that holds the record, and a sync room and a tab on builds from either side of it refuse each other when the tab joins. A sync room an older build stored stays closed until its app moves the room to the new rules.
