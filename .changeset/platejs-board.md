'platejs': patch

Add `platejs/board` and `platejs/board/react`, a whiteboard block that keeps its notes, geo shapes, arrows, frames and pen lines in the document. It has rich text in notes, undo, Suggesting, copy and paste, HTML for the built-in shape types and Yjs sync. Markdown keeps only the shapes' text.

- Insert a board with `tx.plugin(BaseBoardPlugin).insert(props?, { at })`, and a shape into it with its shape plugin, such as `tx.plugin(BaseBoardNotePlugin).insert({ x, y }, { board })`. Each shape gets an id that is unique in the document
- Move, resize, restack and remove shapes by id with the board's `move`, `resize`, `restack` and `remove`, and paste a copied slice of shapes with `insertCopy(slice, { board })`
- Duplicate a board, or shapes in one, with `tx.blocks.duplicate`. The copy's arrows and frames point at the copied shapes. Shapes duplicated inside a board land above every shape of their layer, stepped off their sources in 24px steps, as pasted shapes do, and a duplicated board keeps its shapes where they were
- Style shapes. Each shape type stores the styles that suit it, each with a default: `fill`, `strokeColor`, `dash`, `strokeWidth`, `opacity` and `textSize`. Notes take fill, opacity and text size, geo shapes take all six, arrows all but fill, pen lines outline color and opacity, and frames none. Change a style with `tx.nodes.set` on the shape's key. HTML keeps each style in a `data-board-` attribute, such as `data-board-stroke-width`
- Add your own shape with `defineBoardShape(type, { properties, size, styles })`. `styles` names each board style the shape takes, with its default. The shape gets its id, place, size, stacking, frames, arrow binding, copy and paste and sync from the board
- Render a board with `BoardSurface` and its shapes with `BoardShape`. Pass `height`, such as `'100%'`, to `BoardSurface` to draw that view at a height other than the board's stored one, so a board can fill its page. `useBoardView` and `useBoardActions` read and change the view's camera, tool and next style, which are never stored in the document. `useBoardView` also reads `selectedShapes`, the board's selected shapes with each node as the view shows it, `styles`, each style a pick would set with the value its targets share or `{ mixed: true }`, and `writable`, whether the view can write. `useBoardActions().setStyle(style)` merges `style` into the next style. With the select tool it also writes each style to every selected shape that takes it and to the shape whose text is being edited, as one undo step that keeps the caret. With a drawing tool it styles only the next shape. Mark a top-level toolbar element that sits at the board's side with `data-board-dock="left"` or `"right"`, and `fit` keeps shapes clear of it. When the room beside it cannot hold the shapes at 10 percent zoom, `fit` uses the board's whole width. The mark does not position the element. `useBoardActions` also has `focus`, which moves focus back to the board and keeps its selection, such as after a toolbar menu closes. A shape whose text is being edited ends its edit and stays selected. `useBoardActions` also has `zoomIn`, `zoomOut` and `resetZoom`, which zoom the view as `=` or `+`, `-` and Shift+0 do, in a read-only view too
- Select shapes with a drag on empty board. With the select tool, a mouse or pen drag draws a box that selects every shape wholly inside it, and Shift adds them to the selection. A touch drag on empty board pans, and a middle-button drag, or a drag with Space held while no one types in a shape, pans from any point, over shapes too
- Draw a note, geo shape or frame the size you drag with its tool, over empty board or over other shapes, with the picked outline shown during the drag. A click places the tool's default size centered on the click. Shift keeps a square and Alt centers the box on the press point. On a selected shape's corner handles, Shift keeps its proportions and Alt resizes it around its center
- Line up shapes as you drag them with the select tool. Once the pointer has gone 8px, the selected shapes pull to the nearest edge or center of another shape or frame in view, within 8 screen pixels on each axis, and a line shows each edge or center they share. They never line up with arrows or pen lines. Hold Control or Command to drag freely. An Alt-drag copy lines up the same way
- Pan with the `hand` tool, which `H` picks. A drag with it pans from any point on the board, over shapes and the text being edited too, and in a read-only view. It never selects, moves or edits a shape, and the click after it does nothing. While the hand is picked, the board shows a grab cursor, except over its toolbar and an element with its own cursor. A press with the hand moves focus to the board unless focus is in the text of a shape on that board, so the board's keys work after the drag and a note being edited keeps its edit. When focus sits elsewhere, such as on a toolbar button, a shape still being edited ends its edit and stays selected
- Erase with the `eraser` tool, which `E` picks. A click or drag marks every note, shape, arrow and pen line it passes over, and never a frame, and each marked shape shows `data-erasing`. A geo shape with no fill and no label is marked only where the path passes near its outline, so a pen line drawn inside an empty rectangle goes alone. Release removes them as one undo step. Escape, picking another tool, a cancelled pointer or the view turning read-only mid-drag removes nothing
- Turn on the tool lock to keep the note, shape, frame or arrow tool picked after each shape it adds. Q on the focused board or its selected shapes turns the lock on or off, `useBoardView` reads it as `toolLocked`, `useBoardActions().setToolLocked(locked)` sets it, and the focused board says `messages.toolLock` when it changes. Each view starts with the lock off and never stores it, and Escape and the tool letters change the tool and keep the lock. A shape selected alone shows its resize handles only with the select tool
- Pass every word the board says to `BoardSurface` as its required `messages` prop, typed `BoardMessages`: its label, the instructions a screen reader reads when the board takes focus, and what it says when a shape is selected, a tool is picked, the zoom changes or shapes are removed. The view that holds focus says one message per action through the editor's status region, and `BoardShapeDescription` tells your words a shape's kind, text and, for an arrow, the shapes its ends attach to
- Reach a board from the keyboard through its one Tab stop. Enter there selects the first shape, Tab and Shift+Tab move between its shapes and leave the board past either end, and Tab in a shape's text selects that shape. Enter on a board that the document selects, or that holds the caret, also selects its first shape. On a board with no shapes it moves focus to the board. Escape ends a drag, a connect, a resize or picking in flight and keeps the tool. From a shape's text it selects that shape. Otherwise, on the focused board, it picks the select tool while another tool is picked, and selects the board when the select tool is picked. With the note, shape or frame tool picked, Enter on the board places that shape, selects it and picks the select tool, as a click does, or keeps the tool while the tool lock is on. A note or shape lands 24px right of the selected shapes, or at the view's center when none is selected, and a frame goes around the selected shapes with 24px to spare, or at the view's center. Enter places nothing when a frame around the selected shapes would take in no shape, when no free point is found or when a coordinate would not be a finite number. Mod with an arrow, which is Command on macOS and Control elsewhere, selects the nearest shape in that direction and brings it into view. Mod+D on the board or on selected shapes copies the selected shapes, with the shapes in a selected frame, as one undo step and selects the copies. The copies land as a paste's do, 24px down and right of their sources, or further while another shape sits at their place. A held Mod+D copies once, and with nothing selected a writable board says the selection again. With the arrow tool picked and one shape that is not an arrow selected, Enter starts connecting that shape and says `messages.connecting`. With the arrow tool picked and nothing selected, Enter starts an arrow at the view's center, in whole units. Its start binds there as an arrow key binds it below, and Enter says `messages.starting` with that point and the shape the start binds to, or `null` for a start on no shape. The arrow keys then move the start by 1, or 10 with Shift, binding it where it sits in the frontmost shape under it that is not an arrow, counting a frame only on its edge and a pen line only on its line, or leaving it free, and Tab, Shift+Tab or Mod with an arrow aim it at the middle of a shape that is not an arrow. Each says `messages.aim` or `messages.free` with `side` set to `'start'`. A second Enter keeps the start and says `messages.connecting` with the shape the start binds to, or `null` for a start on no shape, and the end then moves from the start's point. Tab and Shift+Tab, or Mod with an arrow, aim the arrow's end at the middle of another shape that is not an arrow, bring it into view and say `messages.aim`. The arrow keys move the end by 1, or 10 with Shift. It binds where it sits in the frontmost shape under it that is not an arrow or the shape the start binds to, counting a frame only on its edge and a pen line only on its line, and says `messages.aim` with that place as `anchor`. Off every such shape it stays free and says `messages.free`. Mod with an arrow searches from the end's point unless the end sits at a shape's middle. Enter then draws the arrow as one undo step. Enter before an aim or a move, or when the arrow would have no length, as with the end back at the start's point, says `connecting` again, and so does Enter after the shape the end binds to is removed. Escape, a left or middle press on the board, picking another tool, undo, redo, Mod+D, removing the shape the start binds to, the view turning read-only or focus leaving the board ends the connect without drawing an arrow, before or after Enter keeps its start. With one shape that is not an arrow selected, S starts resizing it from its bottom-right corner and says `messages.resizing`. Tab picks the next corner clockwise and Shift+Tab the one before, and the arrow keys move that corner by 1, or 10 with Shift, down to 8 a side, while the opposite corner stays. Each previews the box with no write and says `messages.resize`. Enter writes the box as one undo step when it changed and says the shape again. Escape, a pointer press, the view turning read-only, removing the shape or focus leaving the board puts the box back and writes nothing, and so does any other board key, which then does what it does. Space on the board starts picking shapes when it comes up, in a read-only view too, and says `messages.picking`. A Space press counts only when it goes down with no modifier while no drag, connect or resize runs, and meets no other key or pointer press before it comes up. Tab and Shift+Tab then move a cursor through every shape in stored order and wrap, Mod with an arrow moves it to the nearest shape that way, and Space adds the cursor's shape to the selection or takes it out, each leaving the rest of the selection as it is and saying `messages.pick` with a `BoardPickState`. The shape under the cursor shows `data-board-cursor`. Enter, Escape, a pointer press, focus leaving the board, removing the cursor's shape and any other board key end picking and keep the selection, and that key then does what it does. The corner handles' `data-board-handle` values are the `BoardCorner` names, `topLeft`, `topRight`, `bottomLeft` and `bottomRight`. `=` or `+` zooms in and `-` zooms out one step of 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 400 and 800 percent around the view's center, Shift+0 resets to 100 percent, Shift+1 fits every shape in view, and PageUp and PageDown pan by most of a view, sideways with Shift. Mod with `=` or `-` stays the browser's zoom. Shift+Enter on the board or on selected shapes moves focus to the board's toolbar. Shift+Tab out of the toolbar moves focus back to the board, and so does Escape on a toolbar control unless a popup is open in the toolbar. That Escape keeps the picked tool. Selected shapes stay selected, and a shape whose text was being edited becomes selected. Undo, redo, copy and cut work while shapes are selected. A read-only view keeps the selection keys and copy, and no key there changes the document
- Adding the board changes an editor's schema identity, so a Yjs room written before it refuses the editor. Seed a new room from the old room's document
