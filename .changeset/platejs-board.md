Add `platejs/board` and `platejs/board/react`, a whiteboard block that keeps its notes, geo shapes, arrows, frames and pen lines in the document. It has rich text in notes, undo, Suggesting, copy and paste, HTML for the built-in shape types and Yjs sync. Markdown keeps only the shapes' text.

- Insert a board with `tx.plugin(BaseBoardPlugin).insert(props?, { at })`, and a shape into it with its shape plugin, such as `tx.plugin(BaseBoardNotePlugin).insert({ x, y }, { board })`. Each shape gets an id that is unique in the document
- Move, resize, restack and remove shapes by id with the board's `move`, `resize`, `restack` and `remove`, and paste a copied slice of shapes with `insertCopy(slice, { board })`
- Duplicate a board, or shapes in one, with `tx.blocks.duplicate`. The copy's arrows and frames point at the copied shapes. Shapes duplicated inside a board land above every shape of their layer, stepped off their sources in 24px steps, as pasted shapes do, and a duplicated board keeps its shapes where they were
- Add your own shape with `defineBoardShape(type, options)`. It gets its id, place, size, stacking, frames, arrow binding, copy and paste and sync from the board
- Render a board with `BoardSurface` and its shapes with `BoardShape`. Pass `height`, such as `'100%'`, to `BoardSurface` to draw that view at a height other than the board's stored one, so a board can fill its page. `useBoardView` and `useBoardActions` read and change the view's camera, tool and next style, which are never stored in the document. `useBoardView` also reads `selectedShapes`, the board's selected shapes with each node as the view shows it, and `writable`, whether the view can write. A toolbar uses them to color the selected notes and geo shapes. `useBoardActions` also has `focus`, which moves focus back to the board and keeps its selection, such as after a toolbar menu closes
- Select shapes with a drag on empty board. With the select tool, a mouse or pen drag draws a box that selects every shape wholly inside it, and Shift adds them to the selection. A touch drag on empty board pans, and a middle-button drag, or a drag with Space held while no one types in a shape, pans from any point, over shapes too
- Pass every word the board says to `BoardSurface` as its required `messages` prop, typed `BoardMessages`: its label, the instructions a screen reader reads when the board takes focus, and what it says when a shape is selected, a tool is picked or shapes are removed. The view that holds focus says one message per action through the editor's status region, and `BoardShapeDescription` tells your words a shape's kind, text and, for an arrow, the shapes its ends attach to
- Reach a board from the keyboard through its one Tab stop. Enter there selects the first shape, Tab and Shift+Tab move between its shapes and leave the board past either end, and Tab in a shape's text selects that shape. Shift+Enter on the board or on selected shapes moves focus to the board's toolbar. Shift+Tab out of the toolbar moves focus back to the board, and so does Escape on a toolbar control unless a popup is open in the toolbar. Selected shapes stay selected, and a shape whose text was being edited becomes selected. Undo, redo, copy and cut work while shapes are selected. A read-only view keeps the selection keys and copy, and no key there changes the document
- Adding the board changes an editor's schema identity, so a Yjs room written before it refuses the editor. Seed a new room from the old room's document
